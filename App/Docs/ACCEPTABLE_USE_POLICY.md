# Acceptable Use Policy

_Last updated: 2026-07-13_

This policy governs use of any hosted instance of Live Translate. Operators may
extend it for their deployment.

## You agree NOT to use the service to:

- Violate any applicable law or regulation.
- Infringe intellectual-property or privacy rights of others.
- Submit content you do not have the right to process.
- Upload malware, or attempt to compromise, overload, or disrupt the service
  or its infrastructure (including denial-of-service and excessive automated
  requests beyond configured limits).
- Attempt to bypass authentication, authorization, rate limiting, or access
  other users' private sessions or accounts.
- Harass, threaten, or harm others, or process content that is unlawful,
  abusive, or exploitative.

## Fair use

Automated or bulk usage should respect configured limits
(`MAX_UPLOAD_MB`, `MAX_SESSIONS_PER_USER`, login rate limits). Operators may
throttle or suspend accounts that degrade service for others.

## Enforcement

Operators may remove content and suspend or delete accounts that violate this
policy. Serious violations may be reported to the appropriate authorities.
